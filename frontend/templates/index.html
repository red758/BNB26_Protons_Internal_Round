What a match tells you

  • The file carries an identifier ModelLedger recognises.
  • That identifier points to a record made at registration.
  • Transformed copies can be reconnected when the watermark survives.

What it doesn't

  • No watermark does not mean a person made the file.
  • A watermark alone does not prove which model generated it.
  • Unregistered files are treated as unknown.
